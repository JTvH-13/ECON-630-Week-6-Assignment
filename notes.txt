Choices to confirm:
- SAHIE years in Test 4a: SAHIE includes 2020, but onky kept the same 12 years as Test 1. That way only the outcome changes between the two. 
- Chart 2 groups: it shows only treated and control, not the late expanders, to match the DiD comparison.
- Test 3: to fill the table, used difference in mean changes as the coefficient and the Welch standard error, sqrt(var_t/n_t + var_c/n_c), as the SE.
- Test 4b caveat: the Markdown notes that two-way fixed effects with staggered timing can be biased. It's presented only as a robustness check.
